Wireless EV Charging Station Using Electromagnetic Induction

Project Overview

This project focuses on developing a Wireless Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Station using the principle of electromagnetic induction. It demonstrates how electrical energy can be transferred wirelessly from a transmitter coil to a receiver coil without a direct physical electrical connection.

The system uses a Raspberry Pi Pico W and sensors to detect vehicle presence and monitor electrical parameters during the charging process.

Objectives

- To demonstrate wireless power transfer using electromagnetic induction.
- To detect the presence of an electric vehicle using an IR sensor.
- To monitor voltage and current during the charging process.
- To understand the practical implementation of wireless EV charging.

Hardware Components

1. Raspberry Pi Pico W
2. IR Sensor
3. Voltage Sensor
4. Current Sensor
5. Transmitter Coil
6. Receiver Coil

Working Principle

The project operates based on the principle of electromagnetic induction. When the magnetic flux linked with a receiver coil changes, an electromotive force (EMF) is induced in it.

Working Procedure

1. When a vehicle arrives at the charging station, the IR sensor detects its presence.
2. The sensor sends a signal to the Raspberry Pi Pico W.
3. The microcontroller controls the connected hardware through its interfaced pins.
4. The transmitter coil produces a changing magnetic field.
5. The receiver coil receives the changing magnetic flux and an induced voltage is generated.
6. The voltage and current sensors monitor the electrical parameters during operation.
7. The transferred electrical energy is used for wireless charging.
